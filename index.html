Temozolomide cycle calculator

For adjuvant treatment on days 1–5 of a 28-day cycle.

Use the actual cycle start date, including any previous delay. These dates are scheduling estimates. The next cycle requires laboratory review and clearance by the treating team. Additional blood tests may be needed.

No patient identifiers are needed. Entered dates are not saved or sent to a server by this calculator.

Temozolomide dose calculator

Enter the verified BSA and select the prescribed dose level.

Daily dose = BSA (m²) × selected dose level (mg/m²/day). No BSA cap or capsule rounding is applied.

Arithmetic aid only. Dose selection, escalation, reductions, and treatment clearance require clinical review. Verify the final dose and capsule combination with the treating team and pharmacy. Concurrent treatment uses interruption/discontinuation criteria rather than routine dose reductions.

No patient identifiers are needed. This calculator does not save inputs or send them to a server.

Reference: TEMODAR prescribing information
