Temozolomide cycle calculator

For adjuvant treatment on days 1–5 of a 28-day cycle.

Use the actual cycle start date, including any previous delay. These dates are scheduling estimates. The next cycle requires laboratory review and clearance by the treating team. Additional blood tests may be needed.

No patient identifiers are needed. Entered dates are not saved or sent to a server by this calculator.

Temozolomide dose calculator

Enter the verified BSA and select the prescribed dose level.

Daily dose = BSA (m²) × selected dose level (mg/m²/day). No BSA cap or capsule rounding is applied.

Arithmetic aid only. Dose selection, escalation, reductions, and treatment clearance require clinical review. Verify the final dose and capsule combination with the treating team and pharmacy. Concurrent treatment uses interruption/discontinuation criteria rather than routine dose reductions.

No patient identifiers are needed. This calculator does not save inputs or send them to a server.

Reference: TEMODAR prescribing information

Temozolomide toxicity review

Educational label-based prototype for clinicians. Covers concurrent treatment and review before a subsequent adjuvant cycle. Not for initial treatment clearance, dose escalation, or deciding whether to complete remaining doses of an interrupted adjuvant cycle.

Enter counts in ×10⁹/L: ANC 1.5 means 1,500/µL; platelets 100 means 100,000/µL. Use ANC, not total WBC.

For concurrent treatment, use the lowest counts and worst toxicity during the current concurrent course. For adjuvant treatment, use the cycle being reviewed. Include the current assessment in those extrema.

Liver toxicity must be graded by a clinician using baseline values, laboratory reference ranges, and the applicable CTCAE version. Include other relevant nonhematologic toxicity; exclude alopecia, nausea, and vomiting from these label rules. Unknown grades or incomplete nadir information prevent assessment.

This tool does not issue treatment clearance. Symptoms, infection, bleeding, suspected liver injury, prior permanent discontinuation decisions, and institutional protocols can override these limited criteria. No inputs are saved or transmitted by this calculator.

Source: TEMODAR prescribing information, February 2026, sections 2.1–2.3 and 5.2
